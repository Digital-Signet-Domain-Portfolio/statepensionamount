Payment schedule

Paid every 4 weeks in arrears, so the full new State Pension arrives as £965.20 per payment. The day depends on your NI number. Bank holidays move payments forward to the previous working day.

Payment frequency
Every 4 weeks, in arrears

State Pension is quoted as a weekly figure but paid every 4 weeks, so the amount that actually lands in your account is the weekly rate times four. On the full new State Pension of £241.30 a week that is £965.20 per payment; on the full basic State Pension of £184.90 a week it is £739.601. Your own payment is lower if your NI record does not qualify you for the full rate, so check your figure on the forecast calculator first.

First payment after claiming

The first payment usually arrives up to 5 weeks after the date you reach SPA, and includes the time from SPA to the first scheduled pay day. After that, the 4-weekly cycle settles2.

Bank holidays

If your pay date falls on a bank holiday, payment is made on the previous working day3. This affects the late December / early January period most often.
