Direct answer

The full new State Pension for 2026/27 is £241.30 per week (£12,547.60 per year), paid as £965.20 every 4 weeks2. You need 35 qualifying NI years to get the full amount, and at least 10 to get anything at all3. Your exact figure depends on your NI record and any contracted-out years before April 2016.
