Direct answer

The full new State Pension for 2026/27 is £241.30 per week2. You need 35 qualifying NI years to get the full amount, and at least 10 to get anything at all3. Your exact figure depends on your NI record and any contracted-out years before April 2016.

SERPS calculator: what your Additional State Pension adds now

SERPS (1978 to 2002) and S2P (2002 to 2016) are no longer paid as a separate pension if you reach State Pension age after 5 April 2016. They sit inside your starting amount: if that is above £241.30, the extra is a protected payment paid on top of the full rate4. Enter your weekly figure to split it out.

For anyone reaching State Pension age on or after 6 April 2016. DWP set a starting amount from your record at 5 April 2016: the higher of your old-rules pension (basic plus SERPS and S2P) and your new-rules pension, both after any contracting out.

The part above £241.30 is your protected payment, usually Additional State Pension (SERPS or S2P) built up before April 2016. It rises with CPI, not the triple lock: 3.8% in April 2026, against 4.8% on the full rate.
