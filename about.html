About

A short page so you know who built this, why, and how we make money. Spoiler: not by selling you anything.

Who built this

Oliver Wakefield-Smith, Founder of Digital Signet. I run a small portfolio of UK and US consumer cost / calculator sites. This is one of them, focused on the question I kept being asked at the kitchen table once relatives clocked I worked in tech: how much will I actually get?

What we are not

  • Not affiliated with the DWP, HMRC, or government in any way
  • Not a pension provider or platform
  • Not an IFA or financial adviser (please do not ask us for advice; that is what Pension Wise is for)
  • Not anti-DWP; gov.uk is the source of record and we model around it

How we make money

Honestly: not much, off this site. We have no affiliate links, no adviser referrals, no upsell. There are some unobtrusive context-aware ads on long-form pages and that is the lot. The Digital Signet portfolio includes other sites where the unit economics work better; this one earns its keep by being clean.

Errors and feedback

If you spot something wrong, email oliver at digitalsignet dot com. Errors get fixed within 48 hours and listed on the updates page.
